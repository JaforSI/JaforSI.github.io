Conceptual figures and frameworks from my research on climate change, population dynamics, and health in low- and middle-income countries.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gallery/figure-1.png" alt="Mediation framework linking extreme weather events to fertility outcomes through child mortality risk, livelihood loss, and early marriage">
  <figcaption><b>Pathways from climate extremes to fertility outcomes.</b> A mediation framework showing how cyclones and floods shape fertility preferences and outcomes through child mortality risk, livelihood and landholding loss, and changes in marriage timing and household structure, moderated by vulnerability and access to family planning.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gallery/figure-2.png" alt="Three-country research program spanning Bangladesh, Kenya, and Uganda with shared cross-cutting themes">
  <figcaption><b>A multi-country research program.</b> Field systems across Bangladesh, Kenya, and Uganda, linked by cross-cutting themes of climate vulnerability, population dynamics, health in LMICs, disaster risk reduction, and mixed methods.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gallery/figure-3.png" alt="Climate vulnerability framework with exposure, sensitivity, and adaptive capacity components and their indicators">
  <figcaption><b>A climate vulnerability framework for rural households.</b> Vulnerability as a function of exposure and sensitivity, offset by adaptive capacity, with the household-level indicators used to compare cyclone- and flood-prone areas.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gallery/figure-4.png" alt="Causal pathways from watershed Nature-based Solutions to health outcomes through biophysical, psychosocial, and One Health routes">
  <figcaption><b>Health co-benefits of Nature-based Solutions.</b> Causal pathways from watershed interventions to priority health outcomes through biophysical, psychosocial, and One Health routes, evaluated in the Water Funds Living Lab at the Upper Tana-Nairobi Water Fund, Kenya.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gallery/figure-5.png" alt="Concentric research vision linking climate and human wellbeing to demographic outcomes and cross-cutting research themes">
  <figcaption><b>Research vision.</b> A changing climate at the center of human wellbeing, connected outward through fertility, migration, and mortality to the broader themes of vulnerability, health, disaster risk reduction, and Nature-based Solutions.</figcaption>
</figure>
