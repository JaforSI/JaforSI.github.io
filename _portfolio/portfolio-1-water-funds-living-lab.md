This project evaluates the health co-benefits of Nature-based Solutions (NbS) delivered through watershed interventions. As an interdisciplinary postdoctoral research fellow at the Smithsonian Conservation Biology Institute, embedded with The Nature Conservancy's Water Funds program, I work on how upstream watershed restoration and land-management interventions translate into measurable downstream benefits for human health and wellbeing.

The work combines a causal-pathway framework linking biophysical, psychosocial, and One Health pathways to priority health outcomes, with an evaluation design drawing on survey data, geospatial analysis, and mixed-methods fieldwork.

**Role:** Postdoctoral Research Fellow

**Themes:** Nature-based Solutions, environmental health, watershed management, program evaluation
