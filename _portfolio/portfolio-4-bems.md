As a postdoctoral research fellow at Georgetown University's Institute for the Study of International Migration, I worked on the Bangladesh Environment and Migration Survey (BEMS), examining environmental change, livelihoods, and migration in Bangladesh.

The research explores how environmental stress and livelihood transitions shape migration decisions and in-situ adaptation.

**Role:** Postdoctoral Research Fellow (2023-2024)
**Affiliation:** Institute for the Study of International Migration, Walsh School of Foreign Service, Georgetown University
**Themes:** Environmental migration, adaptation, livelihoods, survey research
